Clinical Operations Guide

Short, practical answers for the most common clinical tasks.

01  Reviewing Your Appointments

The Appointments list shows every booking assigned to you. Update a booking's status to Pending, Done or Completed as care progresses.

02  Calculating a Patient's Bill

Choose Calculate on any appointment to fetch the treatment's pricing and total from the billing service.

03  Issuing an Invoice

Review the calculated amount, then choose Issue Invoice to finalize and save the bill.
