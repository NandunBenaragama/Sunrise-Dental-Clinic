Doctor Portal Operations Guide

My Appointments View

This table displays patient appointments assigned to you. You can change the appointment status dynamically to Pending, Done, or Completed.

Calculating Patient Bill via REST API

Click the 'Calculate' button on any appointment. The system connects to the REST Service to fetch treatment pricing and totals.

Issuing Invoices

Verify the calculated amounts and click 'Issue & Save Invoice Receipt' to finalize billing.
