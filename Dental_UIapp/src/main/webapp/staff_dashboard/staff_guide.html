Operations Guide

Short, practical answers for the most common front-desk tasks.

01  Registering a Doctor

Open Doctors, complete the registration form and submit. This creates the specialist's profile and their portal login in one step.

02  Adding a Treatment

Open Treatments, enter the procedure name and standard fee, then save. It appears immediately in the New Booking treatment list.

03  Booking an Appointment

Open New Booking, look up an existing patient or enter a new one, choose a dentist and treatment, then confirm the booking.

04  Printing a Receipt

From Receipts, choose Print on any invoice to print just that receipt, without the rest of the dashboard.
